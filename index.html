About Me

I study in the Department of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at the University of Dhaka. Prior to my university studies, I attended Notre Dame College for Intermediate Science from July 2022 to June 2024. This platform serves as a central repository for my coursework, lecture notes, and study materials, designed to keep academic resources highly organized and accessible.

My Journey to Becoming an AI/ML Engineer

My transition into artificial intelligence and machine learning is driven by a deep interest in computational modeling and data science.

  • Foundations & Structured Learning: I actively utilize platforms including DataCamp, Coursera, and Kaggle to build my expertise in data science and machine learning.
  • Advanced Frameworks: My technical study involves working with resources like PyTorch and Fastai to understand deep learning applications.
  • Continuous Growth: To accelerate my learning and access advanced computational tools, I subscribed to Google AI Pro in August 2026.
